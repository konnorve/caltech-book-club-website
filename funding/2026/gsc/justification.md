# 2026 GSC Funding Justification

As the treasurer of the Caltech Graduate Book Club, I am writing to request funding support for our 2026 programming. Our proposed budget reflects a sustained commitment to providing an accessible space where graduate students can engage with literature beyond the demands of their research. Over the past year, the book club has grown into a consistent community that supports personal reflection, emotional wellbeing, and open, low-stakes dialogue—experiences that are especially valuable within Caltech’s technical and intensive academic environment.

Ensuring financial accessibility is central to our mission. The cost of purchasing books can be a meaningful barrier for students, and our book subsidies are essential for enabling full and equitable participation. These subsidies, alongside modest food costs for meetings, directly support attendance and help create an environment where students from any division and across all years can participate without financial strain. Our weekly and monthly meeting structure allows students with varying schedules and reading capacities to join at a pace that works for them, further expanding accessibility.

We have read and discussed a wide range of literature, including *One Hundred Years of Solitude*, *For Whom the Bell Tolls*, *Foster*, *Brave New World*, and an array of international short stories. These texts have prompted thoughtful conversations about narrative, culture, history, religion, and ethics, and they have contributed to a shared intellectual and social experience that is rare outside formal coursework. The proposed budget aligns directly with these goals and accurately reflects the expenses required to maintain a club of this scale.

We believe the Caltech Graduate Book Club strongly supports the GSC’s mission to foster community and wellbeing among graduate students. With continued support, we look forward to sustaining and expanding a literary space that has become a meaningful part of student life at Caltech.

Sincerely,

Konnor von Emster
