Expectations & Discussion Guidelines

Expectations

  • Please read to the agreed page, but not beyond
  • Members can opt in and out of individual books
  • When you start a book, try to join for all meetings of that book
  • If you read faster than others, consider picking up another book so that you do not read ahead

Discussion guidelines

Sharing the conversation

  • Don’t talk over people
  • Use the 2×2 rule as a guide: wait two minutes or until two other people have talked before speaking again
  • The 2×2 rule is a suggestion, not a strict requirement

Kindness and open-mindedness

  • Be kind
  • Be open-minded

Privacy

  • Respect others’ privacy
  • Follow the Chatham House Rule: you may discuss ideas from book club outside the group, but don’t identify who said what or attribute personal stories to individuals

For more guidance on thoughtful discussion, see Living Room Conversations’ conversation agreements

Please communicate with the officers if a topic, discussion, or person makes you uncomfortable, and we will try to accommodate you.
