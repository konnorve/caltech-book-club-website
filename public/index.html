About the club

Join & stay connected

Everyone is welcome. You don't need to be affiliated with Caltech to join the club or attend events.

We share updates, event reminders, and coordinate meeting times in our WhatsApp community. Email the officers to request a WhatsApp invitation.

How we read

Weekly books

The weekly group chooses its books, then reads them together in smaller sections: usually about 60 pages a week across 4–6 meetings.

Monthly books

Chosen through quarterly polls. Readers indicate interest at the start of the month, read the book at their own pace, and discuss it at the end of the month. The WhatsApp community helps coordinate meeting times.

Participation & discussions

Reading expectations

  • Opt in or out of individual books.
  • Read to the agreed page, but not beyond.
  • Try to attend every meeting for a book you start.

Discussion guidelines

  • Don't talk over people.
  • Be kind and open-minded.
  • Respect others' privacy.

Finding & funding books

Book subsidies

The club can fund one book per person per term. Funding is limited, so please request a book only if you expect to finish it on time and attend the discussion. Book subsidy requests are collected through a form at the start of each term.

Where to find books

  • Caltech Library
  • Caltech Library Kindle borrowing
  • Pasadena Public Libraries
  • Los Angeles Public Libraries
