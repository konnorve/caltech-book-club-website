01 / Participating

Expectations

Choose your books.
Read along with us.

Join whenever you like

New members are welcome at any time. If a book is already underway, catch up to the agreed stopping point before joining its discussion.

Choose what you read

You can opt in or out of individual books. When you start a book with the group, try to attend all of its meetings.

Keep to the shared reading pace

For books read in sections, please read to the agreed page or chapter, but not beyond. Sharing the same stopping point lets us discover the story together and keeps spoilers out of the conversation.

Finished this week’s reading early? Pick up another book while you wait, so you can keep reading without getting ahead of the group.

02 / In conversation

Discussion guidelines

Different perspectives.
Shared consideration.

Make room for every voice

Listen without talking over others. As a gentle guide, try the 2×2 idea: after speaking, wait about two minutes or until two other people have spoken before jumping back in. It’s a reminder to share the conversation, not a strict rule or a stopwatch.

Be kind and open-minded

Be curious about perspectives different from your own. Ask questions to understand, and disagree with ideas respectfully.

Respect one another’s privacy

Use the Chatham House Rule as our guide: you’re welcome to share ideas from the discussion outside book club, but don’t identify who said what or reveal participants’ identities or affiliations. Don’t retell personal stories in a way that identifies the person who shared them.

More inspiration: Living Room Conversations’ conversation agreements.

We’re here to help

Something making you uncomfortable?

If a topic, discussion, or person makes you uncomfortable, please speak privately with a club officer. We’ll listen and try to accommodate you.
